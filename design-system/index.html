07 — Operating principles

System core. Directed edges.

Consistency protects meaning. Exceptions create atmosphere only when they remain local, accessible, and deliberate.

01

Commerce never waits.

Navigation, product discovery, variant selection, add-to-cart, cart, and checkout work independently of decorative scripts.

02

Native scroll is canonical.

Motion may respond to the browser's scroll position, but it never replaces or hijacks scrolling.

03

Motion has an exit.

Every section initializes once, cleans itself up, and has a reduced-motion equivalent that preserves hierarchy.

04

Break the grid locally.

Oversized type, crop, overlap, and asymmetry belong to authored compositions—not generic component APIs.
